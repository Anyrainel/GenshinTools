# Checkpoint 15: Two Source-Scoped Roles, Four Published Pairs

## Status

This checkpoint adds a generic evaluator for exactly two source-scoped role
records and a real Keqing Lunar-Charged wrapper. It validates only four exact
teams published on the same KQM page. It does not create a role catalog, expand
the role lists into candidate teams, or claim that any team or member is good.

Everything remains under `scripts/guide-factory`. No application, Worker,
production-preset, or distributed-bundle path imports this work. ER remains
deferred.

## Question under test

The empirical question is:

> Can two fully captured source-positive role lists be checked together against several
> explicit same-page teams while preserving unexercised members and refusing
> to infer the remaining pair product?

This is a source-provenance, data-shape, and structural-binding test. It is not
a team-generation or performance experiment.

## Exact source evidence

The indexed KQM Keqing snapshot contributes seven participating records:

- one Lunar-Charged team template;
- one `off-field-hydro-applier` character-role record;
- one `resistance-shred` character-role record; and
- four exact example teams.

All seven are currently observed as `agent-assisted` and `unreviewed` through
the indexed snapshot. The wrapper records each state independently and requires
each participating source record exactly once. Additional unrelated records may
later be added to the same Keqing snapshot without invalidating this sample.
The consolidated template, both role records, and all four target teams must
also remain candidates with `promotionEligible: false`.

The page and every consolidated participating record must use exactly:

`https://keqingmains.com/q/keqing-quickguide/`

A shared but different URL is not accepted merely because all records agree on
it.

the comparison not comparable. Member order is canonicalized and is not a

the shred record. They remain positive source evidence. None of the other 20
possible Hydro/shred pairs is emitted, rejected, scored, ranked, or endorsed.
Any failure in one of the four configured targets withholds all validated pair
observations rather than returning a surviving subset.

## Released-catalog and roster boundaries

The wrapper transiently reuses the checkpoint-12 guide-eligible released
catalog: 125 character IDs and 119 playable identities after 14 special-avatar
forms are withheld. Durable output keeps counts and fingerprints, not the full
catalog.

The current count, full catalog hash, ID-only hash, and playable-identity count
must match the independently checked-in roster-domain report. Same-count
fingerprint drift fails.

The Keqing Lunar-Charged template is then checked at two boundaries:

- a fresh replay through the roster-domain core; and
- the checked-in roster-domain report.

Both must remain `withheld-unresolved-role`. The two source observations are
not installed as a global role resolver.

## Generic evaluator boundary

`sourceScopedRolePairSample.ts` requires exactly two distinct role records on
distinct template slots and at least one explicit target. It fails closed on:

- candidate/promotion-ineligible state, template, slot, role, or role-option drift;
- duplicate role records, slots, target IDs, or exact teams;
- extra unresolved role options outside the configured pair;
- source/page lineage mismatch or ambiguity;
- catalog eligibility or shared playable identity;
- missing member evidence or target membership;
- condition-text acknowledgement or constellation-bound mismatch;
- incomplete or non-structural slot binding; and
- structural multiplicity drift.

Descriptor, target, role-binding, and object-key order do not change stable
output. The evaluator never enumerates a pair product and makes every guide,
recommendation, rank, and global-role claim false.

## What this checkpoint establishes

It establishes that fully captured source-positive lists can survive into a paired
validation step without requiring every member to appear in an exact example
and without converting two lists into an implied candidate domain.

It does not establish:

- exhaustive role membership;
- negative evidence from an omitted exact-team example;
- a reusable global role ontology or resolver;
- team quality, ranking, recommendation, or optimality;
- gameplay execution, aura setup, rotation timing, or buff coverage;
- damage, formula-count, equipment, or stat-allocation quality; or
- an ER requirement.
