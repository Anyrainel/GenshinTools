small Noelle source domain into two partial artifact profiles. It then asks one
narrow computation question: whether the exact source rotation can be

## Rejected calculator representation

The pinned Noelle implementation exposes one `noelle-na` entry labelled
`Q Normal (4-hit)`. Its four parts are an inseparable N1/N2/N3/N4 aggregate.
Both the direct and compiled evaluation paths multiply the complete entry by a
line count, while the replay line provides no formula-part selector. The
calculator's default Noelle combo descriptor contains `noelle-charge` with a
count of three and does not represent the source Normal Attack prefixes.

Every nonzero `noelle-na` count necessarily adds an N4 hit. The source target
requires nonzero N1/N2/N3 hits and zero N4 hits. Therefore neither `N3D`, `N2`,
nor their total can be represented exactly by any nonnegative integer count of
the current aggregate formula.

The computation-representation admission is consequently
`rejected-exact-formula-representation-missing`. No approximation is
substituted, no replay is admitted, and `numericResult` remains null.
