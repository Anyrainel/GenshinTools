## Observed format defect

The reviewed checkpoint-58 packet core is pinned at SHA-256
`defde895365ae4b4b402f66483a04e3e5536d933abc516492e74acf188ed6ef5`.
The audit separates reusable and non-reusable parts:

- the closed field tree, exact field policy, blocker links, and provenance
  hashes are reusable;
- the upstream anchor requires a checkpoint-57 envelope, candidate, and
  artifact profile;
- readiness rejects a packet with no local-relation fields;
- admitted, counterexample, and inconclusive relation states recognize
  checkpoint-57-specific discriminators.

Therefore all six non-Noelle surfaces remain rejected by the current generic
packet format. This is a tested format gap, not an assertion that a generalized
format will work before it is implemented.

  selected applicability report does not serialize the equipment payloads.

  holdouts are additional artifact/plan locators, not weapon or stat payloads.

  set alternatives; weapon, Circlet, and substat evidence remains locator-only.
- Xiao preserves weapon groups, one artifact set, Sands, Goblet, and guarded
  Circlet/substat axes, but its six objects are factory-authored partial
  candidates and the exact roster payload is fragmented upstream.
- Keqing preserves an exact team and rich embedded claims inside two
  factory-authored cross-record compositions; formula-count translations remain
  unreviewed and unapplied, and no rotation/buff-coverage surface is serialized.

## Interpretation boundary

Checkpoint 59 never:

- chooses an equipment option or treats a sole option as selected;
- derives a source rank from serialization order;
- treats request applicability as source authorization;
- treats a holdout locator as a recommendation payload;
- treats a partial candidate or cross-record composition as a source build;
- runs the generator, optimizer, AutoTune, calculator, or rotation replay;
- computes damage, team total, scalar weights, ideal rolls, or ER;
- moves any report or field into production data.

ER is explicitly deferred by user direction. It appears only as one
`scope-deferred` boundary row per subject and is never read as evidence or
computed.
