## Reusable scoped adapter

`formulaPartProjection.ts` operates on one fresh `TeamBuild` catalog. It:

1. requires an existing formula ID, exact owner, exact original part count,
   strictly increasing unique source-part indexes, positive integer hit counts,
   and unique semantic labels;
2. clones the selected source parts without mutating the original entry;
3. retains the existing formula ID so both formula-availability paths continue
   to discover it;
4. temporarily replaces the one local `formulaIndex` entry;
5. awaits the supplied operation; and
6. restores the complete ordered formula index from the original entry
   references in a `finally` block.

The adapter restores the full map even if the callback deletes unrelated
entries, adds entries, and throws. Invalid specifications fail before the first
write.

For Noelle, the projection clones source parts N1, N2, and N3 with effective hit
counts 5, 5, and 3. N4's required zero count is represented by omitting source
part index 3. A zero-hit `FormulaPart` is intentionally not materialized; the
adapter accepts positive hit counts only.

The regular calculator bootstrap still performs its normal one-time,
process-local entity registration. The checkpoint does not claim to reverse
that module initialization. The temporary mutation is limited to the catalog
owned by one fresh offline `TeamBuild`; no published application source,
persisted data, preset, or website bundle is changed.

## Executed technical computation

The executable harness is deliberately smaller than the source team so it
cannot be mistaken for a team replay. Its Guide Factory-authored assumptions
are:

- one level-90 C0 Noelle with 1/1/1 talents;
- R1 White Iron Greatsword;
- no artifact set and an empty artifact stat sheet;
- no combat options, enemy aura, extra buffs, or formula-buff overrides; and
- enemy level 100, 10% resistance, roll multiplier 0.85, and the existing
  `8_6` substat-budget metadata.

The fixture is pinned to released, non-beta data.

None of these choices comes from the source or selects either checkpoint-53
investment candidate.

Before projection, a separate fresh control `TeamBuild` evaluates the four
original parts with effective hits 1/1/1/1. Weighting those direct-evaluator
per-hit results by 5/5/3/0 produces `5267.58051313504`. During the scoped
projection, the interpreted combo path evaluates the three positive parts with
hits 5/5/3 and produces the same total. The compiled path also produces
`5267.58051313504`; its floating-point difference from the interpreted result
is within the declared absolute/relative tolerance. No computed buff override
is present.

This gives three cross-checks on the representation:

- arithmetic over the original four direct-evaluator per-hit results;
- interpreted evaluation of the projected three-part entry; and
- compiled evaluation through the distinct compiler path.

The first check is independent weighting, not an independent damage
implementation.

The second fresh control catalog remains unchanged, and the projected catalog
restores its original entry identity, size, order, and every entry reference.
