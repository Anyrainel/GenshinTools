transition in the offline Guide Factory. It joins the checkpoint-43

This is not a complete build or recommendation. It does not select a weapon,

## Explicit completeness ledger

The contract uses one fixed six-axis character-build vocabulary:

1. weapon;
2. artifact set;
3. Sands main stat;
4. Goblet main stat;
5. Circlet main stat; and
6. substats.

Two axes are present authenticated singletons: artifact set and Goblet. Four
axes are explicitly `missing-not-admitted-no-default`: weapon, Sands, Circlet,
and substats. Missing means absent from this authenticated projection, not
absent from KQM, unsuitable for Xiao, or safe to fill from a nearby record or
current preset.

Energy Recharge is deliberately outside this completeness denominator and is
recorded as `excluded-deferred`. Formula plans, rotations, damage objectives,
generator options, optimizer policy, and player inventory are likewise absent
and unconsumed.

The resulting candidate is not materializable as the repository's complete
guide recommendation schema or runtime artifact-generation candidate. The
join validates only a deterministic singleton field composition; gameplay
compatibility remains unevaluated.
