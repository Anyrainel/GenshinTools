## Captured sheet and allocation domain

- 36 equipment nodes;
- four carry runs per node, for 144 fresh generator captures and 144 distinct
  runtime identities;
- 576 node/carry/character occurrence contexts;
- 21 globally unique generated sheets;

executed for the real default run. This checkpoint performs zero separate
