# Checkpoint 17: Source-Conditioned Candidate Lattice

## Status

This checkpoint projects the validated Keqing Lunar-Charged equipment and stat
claims onto the four exact published teams. It is a classification lattice,
not an optimizer input: it creates no build, multiplies no choice axes, runs no
generator, computes no damage, and uses no ER input.

## Question under test

Can the source repository produce a compact, fail-closed queue of team-specific
equipment and stat evidence without dropping unresolved or unsupported choices
and without turning source-local group labels into a global ranking?

## Source-group boundary

The 42 checkpoint-16 claim units become:

- 13 weapon groups containing 21 weapon claims;
- 6 artifact groups containing 9 artifact claims; and
- 12 independent stat claims: 9 main-stat and 3 substat claims.

The 19 equipment groups retain their source record, recommendation, ordering,
group index, `single` / `alternatives` / `tied` semantics, classification,
conditions, member claims, and search-coverage references. Repeated
Mistsplitter evidence remains separate across its general, traditional-set,
and exceptional-EM contexts.

No weapon, artifact, and stat axes are multiplied together. The durable output
therefore contains zero assembled builds.

## Four-team lattice

Each group or stat claim is projected only onto the four exact KQM teams. The
report contains:

- 76 equipment-group cells;
- 48 stat-claim cells; and
- 168 underlying claim/team rows.

The original claim resolutions remain 70 matched by exact roster and
declared-reaction facts, 8 not matched by those facts, and 90 unresolved.
Equipment rows split further into:

| Condition result | Representable | Not representable |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| matched | 34 | 0 |
| not matched | 8 | 0 |
| unresolved | 62 | 16 |

The 16 unresolved/unrepresentable rows are the same four artifact claims across
four teams: 4pc Thundersoother and the three traditional 2pc combinations.
They remain visible holdouts rather than disappearing from the candidate
boundary.

## Technical-fixture boundary

Only Keqing / Ineffa / Furina / Xilonen has the exact existing formula fixture.
The lattice records that availability but authorizes no comparison:

- the fixture is still an unreviewed authored translation;
- its readiness ledger still has eight blockers;
- the general Mistsplitter claim is matched by exact team facts;
- the fixture's 4pc Thundering Fury anchor remains behind the unresolved
  top-Lunar-Charged-contributor condition; and
- R1 is an experiment assumption while the source refinement is unspecified.

The other three teams explicitly report that no exact formula fixture exists.
No numerical damage, score, rank, or winner is stored.

## Fail-closed behavior

The lattice is set to `null` and all candidate definitions are withheld when
any required invariant changes, including:

- upstream evidence becoming non-comparable;
- missing, duplicate, extra, or reordered exact-team resolutions;
- an unmapped source condition;
- inconsistent members inside one source group;
- an equipment/search-coverage kind mismatch;
- a source refinement being inferred from analyzer candidates;
- a weapon leaving the released native-type-compatible domain;
- the exact Mistsplitter or Thundering Fury anchor changing; or
- the formula fixture moving to another team, becoming reviewed/replay-ready,
  or changing its rotation ID or ordered blocker-code ledger without a new
  checkpoint review.

## What this checkpoint does not establish

- an assembled or legal build;
- cross-record equipment compatibility;
- artifact or weapon ranking;
- stat-weight or ideal-roll values;
- damage, rotation, buff-coverage, or reaction-ownership correctness;
- constellation generalization;
- account-specific availability; or
- an ER target.
