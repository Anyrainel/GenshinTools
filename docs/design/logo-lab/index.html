A6 is the baseline. A7 clarifies the crystal faces within the same outline; A8 also refines the side tips and underside. All three use exactly the same Genshin star and palette.

The design brief

Familiar to players.A four-point gem and an overlapping crystal cluster retain the visual connection to the currency references.

Works without game knowledge.Clear silhouettes, coherent light, and controlled facets should read as deliberate gem emblems without needing a reference explanation.

Independently designed.Custom proportions, facet arrangements, and an adapted rose–violet–blue palette distinguish the marks from the game art. Avoid ornamental detail that only makes sense in the game.

A readable reduction.The 16 px version keeps the silhouette and light–dark balance. Secondary detail belongs in the larger drawing.

Favicon delivery checklist and sources

Square and self-contained.Every SVG has a 64 × 64 viewBox, transparent padding, and no external fonts, images, filters, or animation. The marks are inset so strokes do not touch the canvas edge.

Multiple sizes.PNG exports: 16, 32, 48, 96, and 180 px. SVG exports: full detail and simplified favicon. Use explicit types and sizes in the site’s icon links. MDN icon selection.

Visual acceptance.Compare actual 16 px rasters on light and dark tabs, plus eight colored surfaces. These are design checks, not a universal contrast certification for logos.

Search is separate.Google recommends a square favicon larger than 48 px; the 96 px export covers that size recommendation. Crawlability and stable hosted URLs are deployment checks, not properties of this local study. Google favicon guidance.
