Ten constructions.

Five structural variations on each family: sharp, chamfered, rounded, outlined, and stepped. A shared rose–violet–blue palette keeps the currency connection while making geometry the focus.

Familiar inspiration, independently drawn structure

Keep the visual vocabulary.Four-point stars, crystal clusters, and gem petals preserve the connection to the references. Proportions, tip shapes, shoulders, and facet layouts are newly drawn.

Change the construction.Compare needle tips to clipped ends, angular faces to curved volume, fine rims to drawn contours, and simple surfaces to stepped crowns.

Control the palette.All ten use the same rose, violet, and blue hues. Highlights are less white and cyan; shadows carry more weight. These are structural variants, not recolors.

Judge the small mark.16–24 px artwork retains each variant’s silhouette and defining style while omitting secondary detail. The one-color view isolates the shape.

CONTRAST CHECK

Across the spectrum.

Favicon delivery checklist and sources

Square and self-contained.Every SVG has a 64 × 64 viewBox, transparent padding, and no external fonts, images, filters, or animation. The marks are inset so strokes do not touch the canvas edge.

Multiple sizes.PNG exports: 16, 32, 48, 96, and 180 px. SVG exports: full detail and simplified favicon. Use explicit types and sizes in the site’s icon links. MDN icon selection.

Visual acceptance.Compare actual 16 px rasters on light and dark tabs, plus eight colored surfaces. These are design checks, not a universal contrast certification for logos.

Search is separate.Google recommends a square favicon larger than 48 px; the 96 px export covers that size recommendation. Crawlability and stable hosted URLs are deployment checks, not properties of this local study. Google favicon guidance.
