Two accepted silhouettes. Five finishes each. Peach, enamel, bronze, rose, and garnet bring warmth and a stronger identity to the original currency inspiration.

If the originals were proposed as website logos…

Keep the silhouette.The star, cluster, and petals identify themselves before you see any detail. Preserve their continuous outlines and solid centers.

Reduce the visual noise.Tiny splinters, layered reflections, and central ornaments blur at 16 px. These variants have separate tiny artwork with just four or five broad planes.

Give light a limit.White-cyan highlights wash out on pale tabs and make every surface feel frozen. Warm midtones, deeper shadows, and a restrained rim give the gem substance.

Make the finish distinctive.A currency item should look precious; a logo should also be memorable. These five materials keep the gem vocabulary while adding a recognizable palette and treatment.

Rasterized at the labeled pixel count

16–24 px previews use simplified artwork: fewer faces, a stronger edge, and no fine glints. Larger exports retain the full material detail. “SVG · favicon” and “Try in this tab” use the simplified mark. View at 100% browser zoom to judge native pixel sizes.

CONTRAST CHECK

Across the spectrum.

Favicon delivery checklist and sources

Square and self-contained.Every SVG has a 64 × 64 viewBox, transparent padding, and no external fonts, images, filters, or animation. The marks are inset so strokes do not touch the canvas edge.

Multiple sizes.PNG exports: 16, 32, 48, 96, and 180 px. SVG exports: full detail and simplified favicon. Use explicit types and sizes in the site’s icon links. MDN icon selection.

Visual acceptance.Compare actual 16 px rasters on light and dark tabs, plus eight colored surfaces. These are design checks, not a universal contrast certification for logos.

Search is separate.Google recommends a square favicon larger than 48 px; the 96 px export covers that size recommendation. Crawlability and stable hosted URLs are deployment checks, not properties of this local study. Google favicon guidance.
